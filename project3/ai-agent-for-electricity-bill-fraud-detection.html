About the Project

Intelligent Fraud Detection for Utility Companies

Power distribution companies lose significant revenue due to electricity theft, billing fraud, and abnormal consumption patterns. Manually reviewing thousands of consumer records is slow and inefficient.

Our AI Agent automates fraud detection using machine learning and intelligent data analysis, helping utility companies identify suspicious consumers quickly and accurately.

Electricity theft costs the global economy an estimated $96 billion annually. AI-powered detection can recover up to 60% of lost revenue within the first year of deployment.
